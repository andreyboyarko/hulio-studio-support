Privacy Policy

Last updated October 30, 2026

Your QR and NFC content is processed on your device. Usage analytics is optional and off by default. Network redirect checks and optional iCloud sync involve the external services described below.

The free 1.0 release contains no in-app purchase flow. References to saved projects, QR logos or Premium purchases below apply only to editions that provide those features; they are not offers or features of the free 1.0 release.

Who is responsible

Andrei Boyarko is responsible for QR/NFC Pro Tools & Codes (QR/NFC Pro). Privacy questions: huliostudiosupport@gmail.com. The app has no developer-operated user accounts, advertising tracking or sale of personal data. Its core processing happens on your device; optional sharing and services are described below.

Camera and photos

With camera permission, frames are processed on device to detect codes and are not saved as video or uploaded by the app. You choose photos for local scanning or a QR logo through the system picker; the app does not browse your entire photo library. Saving a PNG to Photos requests add-only permission. Exported images may contain the information encoded in the QR.

Contacts

Choosing a contact uses Apple's system picker and does not request access to the entire address book. Only the selected card's name, phone numbers, email addresses, organization and first website are imported for editing. Contact photos, notes and postal addresses are not imported. You choose which phone numbers and emails to include. A separate action to save a scanned contact to the phone requests system Contacts permission and writes the chosen details.

Current Wi-Fi network and location permission

Only after you tap Current network and confirm the explanation does the app request precise location permission to read the current Wi-Fi name (SSID). It copies only the name, not the password; you enter the password and security type yourself. This Wi-Fi helper does not read or store geographic coordinates or track location in the background. If access is denied, accuracy is reduced or no network is available, manual entry remains available. An SSID you include in a code can be saved or shared with that code.

QR codes and NFC tags

The app processes content you enter, import, scan or read from a tag, including links, text, contact details and Wi-Fi credentials. Writing a tag or sharing a code transfers that content to the tag or recipient. Anyone able to read the code, file or compatible tag may obtain it; QR encoding and NFC writing do not themselves encrypt it. Do not include information you do not want to disclose.

Local storage and optional iCloud

History, notes, favorites and collections are stored in the app's local database. Saved projects, including their content, QR design and any logo, are stored locally, not in the app's iCloud sync. Auto Save Scans controls automatic history saving. If you enable iCloud Sync, the history database uses Apple CloudKit with your Apple account; changes take effect after restarting the app. Apple manages its service and any device backups under your Apple settings and terms.

Links, sharing and network requests

Local link analysis does not contact a reputation database. When Network Redirect Checks are enabled and available, the app sends a direct HEAD request to the URL and follows redirects. Destination sites receive the requested address, your IP address and request headers, including the app's User-Agent; a URL may itself contain personal information. These requests do not pass through a developer-operated server. Opening links or sharing through other apps places the content under the selected service's privacy practices. You can disable redirect checks in Settings.

Purchases in editions that offer Premium

The free 1.0 release has no purchase or restoration flow and does not start StoreKit transaction listening or product loading. In editions that offer Premium, Apple StoreKit processes purchases and restoration. The app reads product and verified transaction information to determine access. The developer does not receive your payment card details through the app. Apple handles billing and its related data under its own terms and privacy policy.

Local diagnostics

The app automatically keeps up to 200 recent local usage events, such as feature names, event times, code types and setting changes. The app does not send this journal to the developer and provides no journal-export feature. Older events are replaced as new ones are recorded. Delete All Data clears this journal. System diagnostic reports and analytics, if shared through your Apple settings, are governed separately by Apple.

Optional Firebase analytics

Usage analytics is off by default. Google Firebase Analytics starts only after you enable Share usage analytics in Settings. Feature events for type selection, verified NFC writing, completed QR sharing, PNG saving and, in editions offering it, Premium views are sent to improve the app. The SDK may also collect session, engagement and purchase events where applicable, timestamps, app and OS versions, device information and a random installation identifier; Google receives your IP address during connections and may derive approximate location.

The app does not send QR/NFC contents, URLs, contacts, SSIDs, passwords, photos or the local journal to analytics. We do not set a User ID; IDFA, IDFV, ad personalization and automatic screen reporting are disabled. Data is pseudonymous, not guaranteed anonymous. Turning analytics off stops collection and resets local Analytics data, but does not automatically delete previously transmitted data. Delete All Data deletes local app data, not Google's data. Google's processing and retention depend on service settings and terms; privacy questions and deletion requests can be sent to huliostudiosupport@gmail.com. Details: Google Privacy Policy and Firebase privacy information.

Retention, deletion and permissions

History and projects remain until you delete them or remove the app's local data. Delete All Data clears history, collections, saved projects, the local event journal and app-managed temporary export files; it resets the current creation screens but does not erase preferences or Apple purchases. With iCloud enabled, database deletions synchronize when Apple's service and connectivity allow; they are not guaranteed to be immediate. Disabling sync does not itself delete cloud copies or backups. Photos, saved phone contacts, exported/shared files and written NFC tags must be managed separately.

Revoke Camera, Photos, Contacts or Location access in iOS Settings; revocation does not erase content already saved. You may contact Andrei Boyarko to exercise applicable privacy rights; local data is managed on your device.

Contacting support

Support is voluntary: huliostudiosupport@gmail.com. The button opens your email app and does not send a message automatically. If you send one, Andrei Boyarko receives your sender address, message and any attachments through Gmail; Google processes mail under its own privacy policy. Correspondence is used to answer and resolve your request and retained only as needed for that purpose or applicable legal obligations. You can request its deletion, subject to those obligations. Do not send passwords or your address book. The app does not automatically attach scan history or diagnostics.
