A little help, right here

Which NFC tags can I use?

NFC features require a compatible iPhone and supported tags. For writing, use a writable NDEF tag with enough space for your content. Locked or read-only tags cannot be overwritten. The app is not a universal reader for every RFID card, access badge or key fob.

My NFC tag won’t read or write. What should I try?

Start the NFC scan in the app and hold the top of your iPhone near the tag. Keep it steady until the operation finishes. Check that the tag is compatible, writable and has enough space. If the issue continues, send us the exact error message and the tag type, if known.

Can I scan a QR code from a photo?

Yes. Open Scan, select Scan from photo, then choose an image containing a QR code or supported barcode. A clear, well-lit image with the entire code visible works best.

How do I save or share a QR code?

Create a QR code in Create → QR Studio, then open its export options. You can save the PNG to Photos or share it using the system share sheet.

Does a link check guarantee a website is safe?

No. Link checks report heuristic risk indicators, not a guarantee of safety. Network checks can inspect redirects when enabled in Settings. Always use caution before entering passwords, personal information or payment details.

Do I need an account or subscription?

The free 1.0 release does not require an app account and contains no purchase flow or subscriptions.
